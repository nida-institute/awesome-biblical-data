machine or into your database.

## Contributing

To suggest a dataset, [open an issue](https://github.com/nida-institute/awesome-biblical-data/issues/new) with:
- Name and brief description
- License
- Where to get it (GitHub URL, download URL, or acquisition process)
- Format(s) available
- Why it meets a bar of scholarly quality

This catalog focuses on resources that are: (a) freely available or clearly
acquirable, (b) of genuine scholarly quality, and (c) useful for computational
work. Survey data, informal concordances, and ecclesial-only resources are
generally out of scope.
